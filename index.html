Movie Board

Movies from TMDb with scores from OMDb. The All tab averages the Rotten Tomatoes critic score, the audience score, the IMDb rating and the box office with equal weight, and only ranks movies that have at least 3 of the 4. Pick a tab to rank by one score, filter by release date in months, and star a movie to keep it handy. Click a poster to watch the trailer.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Ratings data from OMDb; Rotten Tomatoes and IMDb are not affiliated with this site. The audience score is the TMDb user score (Rotten Tomatoes’ Popcornmeter is not available through either API). Box office is OMDb’s domestic gross, or TMDb’s worldwide revenue when OMDb has none, and is scaled from $100K (0) to $1B (100).
